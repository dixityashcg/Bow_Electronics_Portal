## From the practice

- **2026-09-23 · d1-brd** — A client asking for a rule to refuse a request at the door, in a system with no customer identity and no way to reach a customer afterwards, is asking for two things that pull apart: fewer refusals reaching the desk, and fewer arguments. The first is delivered by the rule; the second is not, because an unidentified visitor who is refused can resubmit with a better answer, and the desk that by design never sees the refusal cannot tell the second attempt from an honest one. The refusal also stops being evidence — where a human refusal left a thread, an automated one leaves nothing unless a requirement says otherwise. Two requirements follow and neither is in the request: record every door refusal with the values it was computed from, and treat the refusal screen as the only sentence the organisation gets to say, because nothing reaches the visitor after it. Worth applying wherever an eligibility, entitlement or qualification check is being moved from a person to a form. _(voltway-warranty-claims, note)_
- **2026-09-23 · d1-brd** — A change request that removes a branch from an eligibility rule does not merely simplify it: it moves every piece of configuration that fed the branch from one critical path to another, and it can flip the safe default for an unrecognised value. Where two entitlement periods became one, the classification list that had chosen between them stopped being an input to the customer's answer and became a reporting attribute. Three consequences follow and none is in the request. The list is no longer a go-live blocker, because an empty list still answers every customer correctly, but it becomes the sole blocker for the reporting the change asks for. The rule for an unrecognised value inverts: admitting it was right only while the period was unknowable, and once one period applies to everything, admitting it puts ineligible work on a queue an objective targets at zero. And a period lengthened for claims made from now on silently applies to sales made under the old term, because the system holds the claimed date and not the terms in force at the sale. Worth working through wherever a rule's inputs are reduced rather than added to. _(voltway-warranty-claims, note)_
- **2026-09-23 · d1-brd** — A constraint row that states an absence — no pre-go-live step, no integration, no dependency — is not a finding about the client; it is a prediction about work nobody has designed yet, and it survives only until someone designs it. Here the architecture produced four pre-go-live steps from requirements the BRD had already approved: a host, the network ranges, a sequence number out of the legacy spreadsheet, and a per-person link. None was new scope; each was what an approved requirement costs once designed. Write an absence row with the design still owed, and name who would have to design something for it to stop being true. _(voltway-returns-portal, note)_
- **2026-09-23 · d1-brd** — A supplier's stated cover period and the window in which a buyer can still bring a claim are different lengths, and encoding the first as an automatic refusal quietly disposes of claims that are live under the second. In England and Wales a contract action runs six years from accrual and a sale in the course of a business carries an implied term of satisfactory quality that includes durability, so a two-year commercial term is a term rather than an expiry of liability. Two things follow for any BRD that automates an entitlement cut-off: the refusal has to say what it is a decision about and what it is not, and the period's start has to be pinned to a named event, because in law such cover takes effect on delivery while a client will usually say from purchase — and the two differ by exactly the gap that puts a boundary case on the wrong side. _(voltway-warranty-claims, note)_
- **2026-09-23 · d1-brd** — When an engagement's declared industry bank asks fifteen questions and the pilot's scope answers three, writing the other twelve into Open research with what was tried is more useful than stretching the pack's domain model to cover them. The unanswerable list is what the operator takes to the client, and one of those twelve turned out to be the scope boundary the document most needed. _(voltway-returns-portal, note)_
